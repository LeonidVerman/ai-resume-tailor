- skills_focus_allowlist

Construction rules (deterministic):

A) skills_truth_allowlist (broad truthful allowlist)
- Include all skill_graph.direct_skills.
- Include all related_skills where allowed_usage != forbidden.

B) claim_experience_allowlist (hands-on claims)
- Include all skill_graph.direct_skills.
- Include related_skills where allowed_usage == can_claim_experience.

C) skills_focus_allowlist (JD-focused emphasis)
- Identify JD skill/tool terms that overlap with skills_truth_allowlist.
- Include a compact set (8–20) that best supports jd_top_themes and resume_mode.
- Do NOT add tools not in skills_truth_allowlist.

D) focus_skills_min_count
- director/manager: 6
- principal/staff: 6
- senior/mid/junior: 5
If skills_focus_allowlist has fewer than focus_skills_min_count, set focus_skills_min_count = length(skills_focus_allowlist).

E) cover_letter_tool_allowlist_global
- Conservative union of allowed_tool_mentions across cover_letter_plan proof points.
- Do NOT add tools not in claim_experience_allowlist.

Notes:
- skills_focus_allowlist is for emphasis, not truth gating.
- The writer will be allowed to keep truthful baseline skills even if they are not focus skills.
