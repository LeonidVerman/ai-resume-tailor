[ROLE_LEVEL_TUNING v1.1]

You will receive role_level in the plan or writer packet.

role_level is one of:
director, manager, principal, staff, senior, mid, junior.

Apply the relevant guidance below to BOTH resume and cover letter.
Do not invent facts. Do not change employers, titles, or dates.
Use only supported claims from MASTER_RESUME and CANDIDATE_PROFILE.
If a rule conflicts with required tokens or validation constraints, satisfy tokens first.

GENERAL RULES (all levels)
- Prefer concrete outcomes and mechanisms over generic adjectives.
- Keep claims grounded and specific.
- Do not add unsupported tools, metrics, domains, or responsibilities.
- Keep thin roles concise and factual.
- Mechanisms may be included, but the framing must match role level.

MECHANISM FRAMING RULE (all levels)
If a mechanism phrase is used, keep it verbatim if required by the writer packet,
but adjust surrounding framing to match role level:
- Director: "Led / drove / established / set direction for ... including <mechanism>"
- Manager: "Led the team to deliver ... including <mechanism>" or "Guided implementation ... including <mechanism>"
- Principal: "Defined architecture / standards ... including <mechanism>"
- Staff: "Designed / led technical direction ... using <mechanism>"
- Senior: "Designed / implemented ... using <mechanism>"
- Mid: "Built / improved ... using <mechanism>"
- Junior: "Contributed to / supported ... using <mechanism>"

Do not imply ownership beyond the role level.

DIRECTOR LEVEL
Primary objective: leadership, strategy, execution, org impact.
Tone: executive but concrete. Credibility comes from outcomes and decisions.
Resume emphasis:
- Lead with leadership scope and business outcomes.
- Frame mechanisms as architectural direction and platform evolution, not hands-on implementation.
- Show cross-team collaboration and operational maturity (tradeoffs, reliability, delivery predictability).
- Mention hiring, mentoring, process improvement only if supported.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with leadership verbs: Led, Directed, Drove, Established, Owned, Scaled.
Avoid:
- Inflating scope (P&L, company-wide strategy) unless explicitly supported.

MANAGER LEVEL
Primary objective: people leadership + delivery accountability + technical judgment.
Tone: practical, delivery-oriented, grounded.
Resume emphasis:
- Make team leadership explicit: coaching/mentoring, setting standards, improving execution.
- Include delivery language when supported: backlog, milestones, timelines, risk management, cross-functional alignment.
- Include mechanisms when they support credibility, but do not stack multiple mechanisms into one sentence.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with: Led, Managed, Mentored, Guided, Coordinated, Partnered, Improved.
Avoid:
- Director-level strategy inflation.
- Pure IC tone unless sources strongly indicate hands-on coding; even then, keep it brief.

PRINCIPAL LEVEL
Primary objective: org-wide technical direction without people-management claims unless supported.
Tone: technical strategy, standards, architecture governance.
Resume emphasis:
- Architecture roadmaps, reference patterns, platform consistency, reliability standards.
- Cross-team technical influence and decision-making.
- Use mechanisms as exemplars of architectural direction.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with: Defined, Established, Standardized, Designed, Led (technical), Guided, Drove.
Avoid:
- Claiming people management or delivery ownership unless supported.

STAFF LEVEL
Primary objective: high-leverage technical leadership across teams.
Tone: hands-on architecture + influence.
Resume emphasis:
- Solving ambiguous problems, leading complex initiatives, mentoring engineers.
- Use mechanisms to show depth and how outcomes were achieved.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with: Led, Designed, Drove, Implemented, Mentored, Delivered.
Avoid:
- Director-level org claims.

SENIOR LEVEL
Primary objective: architecture depth, ownership of complex systems, high-leverage execution.
Tone: confident, technical, outcome-driven.
Resume emphasis:
- Distributed systems, scalability, reliability, performance, integration experience.
- Leadership is secondary but present (mentoring, leading projects).
Avoid:
- Generic "scalable platform" without mechanisms.
- Inflating people management beyond sources.

MID LEVEL
Primary objective: strong execution, growing ownership, reliable delivery.
Tone: clear, practical, results-focused.

JUNIOR LEVEL
Primary objective: capability, learning velocity, dependable contribution.
Tone: humble but confident, concrete.
