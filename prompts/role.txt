Apply the relevant guidance below to BOTH resume and cover letter.

GENERAL RULES (all levels)
- Prefer concrete outcomes and mechanisms over generic adjectives.
- Keep claims grounded and specific.
- Do not add unsupported tools, metrics, domains, or responsibilities.
- Keep thin roles concise and factual.
- Mechanisms may be included, but the framing must match role level.

MECHANISM FRAMING RULE (all levels)
If a mechanism phrase is used, keep it verbatim if required by the writer packet,
but adjust surrounding framing to match role level:
- Director: "Led / drove / established / set direction for ... including <mechanism>"
- Senior: "Designed / implemented ... using <mechanism>"
- Mid: "Built / improved ... using <mechanism>"
- Junior: "Contributed to / supported ... using <mechanism>"

Do not imply ownership beyond the role level.

DIRECTOR LEVEL
Primary objective: leadership, strategy, execution, org impact.
Tone: executive but concrete. Credibility comes from outcomes and decisions.
Resume emphasis:
- Lead with leadership scope and business outcomes.
- Use metrics to show impact (delivery, reliability, scaling, speed, quality).
- Frame mechanisms as architectural direction and platform evolution, not hands-on implementation.
- Show cross-team collaboration and operational maturity (reliability, incident response, tradeoffs).
- Mention hiring, mentoring, process improvement, and delivery predictability only if supported.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with leadership verbs: Led, Directed, Drove, Established, Owned, Scaled.
- Include 1 mechanism clause when it strengthens credibility, but avoid stacking multiple mechanisms in one bullet.
Avoid:
- IC-only tone like "implemented X in code" unless the sources explicitly show hands-on work and you choose to mention it briefly.
- Overclaiming executive strategy, P&L, company-wide AI transformation, or roadmap ownership unless supported.
Cover letter emphasis:
- Connect leadership, delivery, and platform evolution to the company needs.
- Mention 1 to 2 mechanisms as examples of how you guide systems and teams.

SENIOR LEVEL
Primary objective: architecture depth, ownership of complex systems, high-leverage execution.
Tone: confident, technical, outcome-driven.
Resume emphasis:
- Lead with distributed systems, scalability, reliability, performance, integration experience.
- Use mechanisms to explain how outcomes were achieved.
- Show ownership: designed components, drove initiatives, set standards, mentored engineers.
- Leadership is secondary but present (mentoring, leading projects).
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with technical ownership verbs: Designed, Built, Implemented, Optimized, Led, Delivered.
- Include 2 to 3 mechanism clauses across the role, distributed across bullets.
Avoid:
- Purely generic phrases like "scalable architecture" without mechanisms.
- Inflating people management beyond sources.
Cover letter emphasis:
- Connect mechanisms and ownership to the job themes.
- Show collaboration and reliability mindset.

MID LEVEL
Primary objective: strong execution, growing ownership, reliable delivery.
Tone: clear, practical, results-focused.
Resume emphasis:
- Emphasize contributions to core systems, feature delivery, performance improvements, and reliability.
- Include mechanisms sparingly and only where supported.
- Show progression: increasing ownership, improving systems, collaborating effectively.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with: Built, Improved, Delivered, Automated, Refactored, Supported.
- Use 1 to 2 mechanisms across the role, not in every bullet.
Avoid:
- Over-framing as a system architect unless sources support it.
- Heavy leadership framing.
Cover letter emphasis:
- Focus on execution strength, collaboration, learning, and reliability.

JUNIOR LEVEL
Primary objective: capability, learning velocity, dependable contribution.
Tone: humble but confident, concrete.
Resume emphasis:
- Highlight clearly supported tasks and outcomes (bug fixes, features, tests, documentation, tooling).
- Mention collaboration, mentorship received, and growth (only if supported).
- Mechanisms should be minimal and simple.
Bullet style:
- Start bullets with: Contributed to, Assisted with, Implemented, Tested, Documented, Supported.
- Use at most 1 mechanism phrase per role, only if supported and simple.
Avoid:
- Claims of owning architecture, leading teams, or large-scale responsibility unless explicitly supported.
Cover letter emphasis:
- Focus on motivation, learning, teamwork, and delivering well-scoped results.
