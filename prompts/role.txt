[ROLE_LEVEL_TUNING v2.0]

You will receive role_level.

role_level is one of:
director, manager, principal, staff, senior, mid, junior.

Apply guidance to BOTH resume and cover letter.

GENERAL RULES
- Prefer concrete artifacts and outcomes over adjectives.
- Avoid generic phrases unless followed by specific system or process detail.
- Avoid stacking multiple mechanisms in one sentence.
- Avoid vague claims like “mission-critical systems” without specifics.
- Do not inflate scope beyond evidence.

GENERIC LANGUAGE SUPPRESSION
Do not use:
- scalable platform
- robust system
- cutting-edge
- innovative solution
unless followed by specific mechanism and measurable outcome.

MECHANISM DEPTH RULE
Every mechanism bullet must include:
- The mechanism itself
- The system boundary or component it affected
- A constraint, tradeoff, or context
- A measurable or observable outcome (if supported)

---------------------------------------------------------------------

DIRECTOR

Focus:
- Organizational impact
- Strategy
- Cross-team coordination
- Delivery governance

Artifact Enforcement (MANDATORY if supported by evidence):
Include at least 3 of the following artifact types across the resume:

1) Delivery Governance Artifact
   - Release cadence definition
   - Process transformation
   - Quality or predictability improvement
   - Escalation framework
   - Risk management mechanism

2) Organizational Leadership Artifact
   - Hiring decisions or headcount growth
   - Team structure evolution
   - Mentorship or leadership development program
   - Performance management system

3) Cross-Functional Alignment Artifact
   - Roadmap alignment with Product
   - Executive reporting
   - Dependency coordination across teams
   - Stakeholder negotiation

4) Strategic Technical Direction Artifact
   - Platform standardization
   - Architectural direction setting
   - Long-term modernization initiative
   - Governance of shared components

Rules:
- Do not describe leadership abstractly.
- At least one artifact must include measurable impact or observable change.
- Mechanisms must support strategic direction, not implementation detail.

---------------------------------------------------------------------

MANAGER

Focus:
- Team execution
- Delivery ownership
- People development
- Operational reliability

Artifact Enforcement (MANDATORY if supported by evidence):
Include at least 3 of the following artifact types across the resume:

1) Delivery Execution Artifact
   - Sprint planning ownership
   - Milestone delivery
   - Incident leadership
   - Backlog prioritization
   - Reduction of spillover or missed commitments

2) People Development Artifact
   - Hiring participation
   - Mentoring engineers
   - Performance reviews
   - Career growth guidance
   - Onboarding improvements

3) Cross-Functional Coordination Artifact
   - Collaboration with Product or Design
   - Stakeholder updates
   - Risk communication
   - Requirement clarification cycles

4) Operational Improvement Artifact
   - Process optimization
   - Test coverage increase
   - Deployment pipeline improvement
   - Observability or reliability enhancement

Rules:
- At least one artifact must show execution accountability (not just participation).
- At least one artifact must show people leadership.
- Mechanisms should reinforce delivery credibility, not dominate.

---------------------------------------------------------------------

PRINCIPAL

Focus:
- Architecture standards
- Cross-team influence
- Technical leadership without direct org management

Artifact Expectations:
Include at least 2 of:
- Architecture review process
- Cross-team standard adoption
- Design RFC leadership
- System boundary clarification
- Platform-level design tradeoff

Mechanisms must illustrate architectural direction.

---------------------------------------------------------------------

STAFF

Focus:
- High-leverage execution
- Mentorship
- Influence within team or domain

Include at least:
- 1 cross-team contribution
- 1 technical depth artifact
- 1 reliability or scalability artifact

---------------------------------------------------------------------

SENIOR

Focus:
- Strong technical ownership

Include:
- 1 significant technical initiative
- 1 performance or reliability improvement

MID / JUNIOR

- Learning velocity

Mechanisms framed as contribution, not strategy.

FINAL CHECK
- Tone matches role_level.
- Artifacts are concrete, not abstract.
- No scope inflation.
- Mechanisms are contextual and relevant.
- Leadership claims are supported by operational evidence.
