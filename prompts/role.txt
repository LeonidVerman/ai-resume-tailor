[ROLE_LEVEL_TUNING v1.2]

You will receive role_level.

role_level is one of:
director, manager, principal, staff, senior, mid, junior.

Apply guidance to BOTH resume and cover letter.

GENERAL RULES
- Prefer concrete mechanisms and outcomes over adjectives.
- Avoid generic phrases unless followed by specific system detail.
- Avoid stacking multiple mechanisms in one sentence.
- Avoid vague claims like “mission-critical systems” without specifics.

GENERIC LANGUAGE SUPPRESSION
Do not use:
- scalable platform
- robust system
- cutting-edge
- innovative solution
unless followed by specific mechanism and measurable outcome.

MECHANISM DEPTH RULE
Every mechanism bullet must include:
- The mechanism itself
- The system boundary or component it affected
- A constraint or tradeoff
- A measurable or observable outcome (if supported)

DIRECTOR
- Emphasize strategy, org impact, cross-team alignment.
- Include hiring, mentoring, delivery governance if supported.
- Frame mechanisms as architectural direction, not implementation.
- Include at least one bullet about delivery improvement if supported.

MANAGER
- Make team leadership explicit.
- Include delivery language: roadmap, milestones, risk, cross-functional coordination (if supported).
- Include at least:
  - 1 people development or mentorship signal
  - 1 delivery process or execution improvement signal
- Mechanisms support credibility but are not stacked.

PRINCIPAL
- Emphasize standards, architectural governance, cross-team influence.
- Use mechanisms as exemplars of design direction.

STAFF
- High-leverage execution + influence.
- Mix architecture depth and mentorship.

SENIOR
- Architecture depth dominates.
- Mechanisms + performance + reliability.
- Leadership secondary but visible.

MID / JUNIOR
- Focus on execution and learning velocity.
- Mechanisms framed as contribution, not ownership.

FINAL CHECK
- Tone matches role_level.
- No scope inflation.
- Mechanisms are natural and contextual.
