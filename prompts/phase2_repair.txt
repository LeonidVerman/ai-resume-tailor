REPAIR PRINCIPLES (quality-critical)
1) Repairs must read naturally and remain logically consistent.
2) When adding an architectural mechanism, INTEGRATE it into the bullet’s meaning:
   - The mechanism must plausibly explain HOW the outcome was achieved.
   - Prefer rewriting the bullet to incorporate the mechanism.
   - Avoid tacked-on trailing clauses ("... via X") unless X truly describes the method.
3) Choose relevant mechanisms:
   - Performance/scale bullets -> caching, replicas, scaling, queues, etc.
   - Reliability/resilience bullets -> timeouts, circuit breakers, retries, isolation, etc.
   - Developer productivity/process bullets -> code review, CI/CD, testing, documentation, AI tooling (NOT database replicas).
4) Keep edits targeted. Do not rewrite the entire resume.
